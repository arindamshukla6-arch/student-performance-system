About this project

AI-Based Student Performance Prediction and Early Warning System, a B.Tech mini project prototype.

What it does

Mentors enter four academic inputs for a student: attendance, internal assessment marks, assignment status and previous academic performance. A machine learning model screens the student as Low, Moderate or High risk and suggests support actions.

The goal is to notice students who may need help early, while there is still time to act.

How it works

  1. A synthetic dataset is generated and cleaned.
  2. Missing values are filled in and categories are encoded in a scikit-learn pipeline.
  3. Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest are trained and compared.
  4. The model with the best cross-validated macro F1 is saved with Joblib.
  5. Flask loads that model and predicts for each form submission.

Limitations of this prototype

  • All data is synthetic. No real student records are included.
  • Model scores describe the synthetic data only, not real-world accuracy.
  • Only four inputs are used. Many real factors are not captured.
  • The login is one shared demo account, with no roles or audit trail.
  • It is not ready for institutional use. Real deployment would need consent, privacy review, proper authentication and testing for bias.
Human review required. A prediction is a screening indication, not a judgement about a student. Decisions should always be made by teachers and mentors who know the student.
